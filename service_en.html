Canadian Digital Service Service Inventory Lookup Service Inventory Lookup This is a prototype. It uses the GC Service Inventory, published by TBS on open.canada.ca. It covers services in that inventory year only. The GC Service Inventory is a list of all of the services the Government of Canada provides to the public, both organizations and individuals. If your work supports a service in the inventory, you can search for it by entering your department and searching for your service below. Looking for a program code instead?
